    The Flower Symphony is an immersive artistic installation and live audio performance created through a collaboration between paper artist Aurély Cerise and interactive design team Can't Touch This Studio. Exploring the phenomenon of synesthesia, the project creates an intimate connection between delicate paper craft, interactive technology, and human touch.


    Each individual flower in the installation is handcrafted from paper and rendered conductive through integrated technology. When touched, the natural electrical conductivity of the human body activates the sculptures, prompting each flower to play a specific, organic musical note composed in collaboration with musician Olga Maximova. Because the sound is generated dynamically through tactile interaction, participants can create an infinite variety of musical compositions simply by touching the floral display.


    By combining traditional paper art with sensory technology and sound design, The Flower Symphony translates physical touch into a live auditory experience, turning handcrafted visual art into an interactive instrument.
