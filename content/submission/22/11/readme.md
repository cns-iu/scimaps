    The FluentPet system is a pet communication framework designed to enable both dogs and cats to express themselves using recordable sound buttons. Animals activate these low-resistance buttons—requiring only about 200 to 250 grams of pressure—using either a paw or a nose. Owners assign specific words to each button, ranging from foundational needs like "food," "water," and "outside" to relational or expressive concepts such as "play," "pain," "help," "yes," "no," "mom," and "dad." The modular tile layout scales smoothly from a small initial setup to expansive boards containing dozens of concepts.


    With the newer FluentPet Connect system, button presses are automatically logged and transmitted via Wi-Fi to a dedicated mobile application. The app tracks press activity in real time, enabling owners to analyze vocabulary trends, observe behavioral patterns, and monitor household communication history over time. Backed by over 50 million reported button presses, thousands of user reviews, and independent 2026 evaluations ranking it as the best overall pet communication-button system, FluentPet offers a structured, data-informed platform for tracking interspecies communication.
