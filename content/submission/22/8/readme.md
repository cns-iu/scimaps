    The Marble Skill Taxonomy interactive is an open, structured visual map of primary and elementary education produced by Marble. Rather than presenting curriculum data as a flat list, the tool organizes learning into an interconnected graph of 1,590 micro-topics—individual teachable ideas spanning subjects like Science, Mathematics, English, History, Personal and Social Development, Life Skills, Computing, and Learning to Learn.


    This interactive display renders each micro-topic as a color-coded dot organized vertically by age, with connecting threads representing prerequisites. Selecting any concept allows users to dynamically trace every underlying topic a student must master beforehand. Each micro-topic entry includes a plain-language description, mastery evidence criteria, age ranges, and classification by learning type, including conceptual, procedural, representational, language, or meta.


    Underlying the map is a directed acyclic graph featuring 3,221 prerequisite dependencies, with each link designated as a hard or soft requirement accompanied by a brief explanation. To bridge theory and established educational frameworks, micro-topics align directly with national curriculum standards, including the Common Core, NGSS, and the UK National Curriculum. The taxonomy also features 183 parent-friendly domain summaries grouped by subject, domain, and age band, offering a clear, navigable representation of childhood skill progression.
